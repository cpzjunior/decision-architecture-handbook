Contribute only changes that improve the Decision Architecture Handbook's accuracy, clarity, consistency, or usefulness.

Preserve the project's purpose: explain decision-making concepts without prescribing a universal methodology.

When editing:

* Preserve existing meaning unless intentionally correcting it.
* Prefer concise, precise language.
* Make assumptions, context, constraints, uncertainty, and trade-offs explicit.
* Distinguish concepts, methods, opinions, and factual claims.
* Avoid unsupported claims, unnecessary terminology, and redundant rewrites.
* Use concrete examples and reliable references when they add value.
* Avoid presenting context-dependent practices as universal rules.
* Maintain consistency with existing terminology and concepts.

For significant conceptual changes, explain the rationale and check consistency with the rest of the handbook before implementing them.

The maintainer has final editorial authority.
