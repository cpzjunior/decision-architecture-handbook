# Contributing

Contributions to the Decision Architecture Guide are welcome.

The project is open and evolving. Contributions may include improvements to existing content, new concepts, practical examples, references, translations, corrections, and critical perspectives.

## Issues

Use issues to propose ideas, discuss problems, or suggest significant changes.

When possible, provide:

* Context and motivation
* The proposed change
* Relevant examples or references

## Pull Requests

Keep pull requests focused and explain what was changed and why.

For significant changes, open an issue first to discuss the proposal.

## Content Guidelines

Contributions should:

* Focus on concepts rather than prescribing a universal methodology.
* Make context, assumptions, and constraints explicit.
* Distinguish concepts from specific frameworks or methods.
* Make uncertainty and trade-offs clear when relevant.
* Prefer concrete examples and reliable references.

The goal is to develop a common language for understanding decisions across different contexts, not to create another universal framework.

## Discussions

Different perspectives are welcome. Critique ideas, assumptions, and arguments rather than individuals, and keep discussions constructive and evidence-based.
